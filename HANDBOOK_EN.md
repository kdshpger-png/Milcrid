# Milcrid – English Handbook

> **Milcrid currently speaks German.** Everything you see on screen – buttons, windows, messages – is in German,
> and the AI listens and answers in German. This handbook explains Milcrid in English and translates the German
> words you will meet. **An English version of Milcrid is in progress.**
>
> Throughout this handbook, German screen words are shown in **bold** with the English meaning in brackets,
> e.g. **Einstellungen** (Settings). Sentences you can *say* to Milcrid are given in German, with the meaning next to them.

*Status: September 2026. Milcrid is under active development – if something looks different from this handbook,
the screen is right.*

## 2. What is Milcrid?

| | |
|---|---|
| What | A user interface for the whole PC – with its own AI |
| AI | runs on your own computer, without the internet |
| Control | mouse, keyboard and voice |
| Language | German (English in progress) |
| Based on | Linux (Ubuntu) |
| Developed by | Klaus, together with a team of AIs |
| More | [miluh.de](https://miluh.de/) |

**Milcrid** is a user interface for your computer in which **its own artificial intelligence** works alongside you.
Instead of an ordinary desktop with a start menu, you see the **Milcrid home screen**: big buttons, an input field,
and windows for everything you do. You can click as usual – or simply tell Milcrid what should happen.

The AI runs **on your own PC**. What you say and write stays with you.

> "KI braucht kein Ich. Sie braucht Dich für das Wir." – *AI doesn't need an "I". It needs you for the "we".*

Most AI programs run on other people's computers on the internet: you send your questions away and get answers
back. Milcrid goes the other way. The AI – a so-called **language model** – is installed on your computer and
works there. That's why it can also **do things on your PC**: open programs, arrange windows, find files, enter an
appointment, set the volume.

The idea is in the motto above: the AI should not act instead of you, but **together with you**. You decide, it
helps. For anything that cannot be undone – like shutting down the PC or deleting a file – Milcrid asks first.

### What Milcrid can do

- **Talk and write with you** – answer questions, explain texts, collect ideas.
- **Operate the PC** – open programs, enlarge windows, arrange windows side by side.
- **Bring its own small apps:** clock, calculator, appointments, notes, calendar, sketch, editor, image viewer, video.
- **Manage files** in the file manager and in **Themen** (themes) – your own collections.
- **Remember** how you say things – and learn when you tell it what was right.
- **Be honest:** Milcrid checks whether the AI really did what it claims – and tells you if it didn't.

## 4. Talking and writing to Milcrid

**Writing:** click the input field at the bottom, type what you want and press Enter – or the arrow on the right of
the field. The answer appears in the chat window.

**Speaking with the microphone:** **hold down** the microphone button in the input field, speak, and let go. Milcrid
writes what you said into the field and sends it, just like typing. Recording stops by itself after 30 seconds.

**Speaking with the wake word:** when the **Codewort** (wake word) is switched on, Milcrid listens without you
pressing a button. Say the wake word first and then your wish, for example: *„Computer, öffne die Uhr."* (Computer,
open the clock). You can choose the wake word yourself under **Lokale KI › Fähigkeiten** (Local AI › Abilities),
**„Codewort ändern"**. There you also switch the microphone and the wake word on and off separately.

**Chat and font size:** small buttons on the right of the input field: **− • +** make the text smaller, normal or
larger; **○ 1 2 3** set the chat window to off, small, medium or full screen.

## 5. Things you can say (German, with meaning)

You don't have to learn fixed commands – say it the way you would say it to a person (in German). If something is
unclear, Milcrid asks instead of guessing.

| You say (German) | Meaning | What Milcrid does |
|---|---|---|
| öffne Firefox | open Firefox | starts Firefox |
| öffne die Uhr · öffne den Editor | open the clock · open the editor | opens Milcrid's own apps |
| mach die Uhr groß · minimiere den Kalender | make the clock big · minimize the calendar | changes a window |
| schließe alle Fenster | close all windows | tidies the screen – minimized windows in the icon window stay |
| schließe alle Fenster und Icon Fenster | close all windows and the icon window | closes everything, including minimized windows |
| ordne alle Fenster nebeneinander an | arrange all windows side by side | arranges the open windows |
| öffne Thema Haushalt | open theme "Haushalt" (household) | opens one of your themes |
| notiere: Milch kaufen | note: buy milk | creates a note |
| stell die Lautstärke auf 30 Prozent | set the volume to 30 percent | changes the volume |
| wie spät ist es? | what time is it? | tells time and date |
| ist die Uhr offen? | is the clock open? | answers without doing anything |
| was hast du gerade eben gemacht? | what did you just do? | says what it really executed last |
| such im Internet nach … | search the internet for … | searches online (only when you say "Internet") |
| PC Neustart · PC aus | PC restart · PC off | opens the confirmation window – only your „ja" (yes) or a click executes it |
| speicher den Chat | save the chat | saves the conversation to Milcrid's memory |
| das war richtig | that was right | Milcrid remembers your sentence (see below) |
| das ist ein Test · Test Ende | this is a test · test end | marks a test; Milcrid learns nothing in between |

### Milcrid learns: thumbs, memory list, words

- Under every answer there are **👍 and 👎**. Use them to tell Milcrid whether it was right. With 👎 a screenshot is
  taken as well, so the mistake can be found later.
- If you say **„das war richtig"** (that was right) directly after a command was carried out, Milcrid stores your
  sentence in the **Merkliste** (memory list). Next time it carries out exactly this sentence immediately – without
  thinking. A plain „ja" (yes) does **not** teach it anything.
- If speech recognition keeps mishearing a word, add it to the **Wörterliste** (word list) under
  **C26SO › 3 · Spracheingabe**: on the left what arrives, on the right what you meant.
- What Milcrid did not understand at all is collected under **Lokale KI › Fähigkeiten › Nicht verstanden**.

## 6. Windows

Everything in Milcrid opens in **windows** that you can move freely:

- **Move:** drag the title bar.
- **Resize:** drag the bottom right corner.
- **— Minimize:** the window folds away and appears as a small picture in the **icon window**.
- **⤢ Maximize:** the window fills the screen. Click again – normal size.
- **× Close.**

**The icon window:** as soon as you minimize a window, the icon window appears at the bottom left with a picture for
it. Click the picture to bring the window back. You can move and resize the icon window. The round blue button in it
closes everything kept there. You can also say *„öffne Icon Fenster"* / *„schließe Icon Fenster"* (open / close icon
window).

**Arranging windows:** with several windows open, **right-click a free spot** on the home screen: arrange them side by
side, one below the other, or cascaded. Or say *„ordne alle Fenster nebeneinander an"*.

## 7. Your own shortcuts: blue buttons and golden dots

The blue buttons at the edges and the golden dots at the bottom are your **shortcuts** – except the blue **Portal**
button at the bottom right, which always opens the Portal. To assign one: drag a program, a theme or an area with the
mouse onto a **free** button or dot. From then on, one click opens exactly that.

- **Free it again:** right-click the button › **„Verbindung trennen"** (disconnect).
- **How many there are:** **Einstellungen › Buttons** (Settings › Buttons) – number of blue buttons and golden dots per side.
- The two outermost golden dots are fixed: **Neustart** (restart, left) and **Ausschalten** (shut down, right).

| App | What for |

| 🕐 Milcrid Uhr | time, alarm, timer, stopwatch |
| 🧮 Milcrid Rechner | calculator |
| 🗓️ Milcrid Termine | appointments with reminders, files and notes |
| 📝 Milcrid Notizen | notes – also for a date or an appointment |
| 📅 Milcrid Kalender | monthly overview of appointments |
| 🎨 Milcrid Skizze | draw, and show the AI what it should become |
| 📝 Milcrid Editor | quickly write something – or open text and program files |
| 🖼️ Milcrid Bildbetrachter | view, browse, rotate, save images |
| 🎬 Milcrid Video | play videos and music |
| 🌐 Milcrid Online KI | chat with an internet AI (Gemini, Claude …) using your own access key |
| ▶️ Milcrid Direkt Aufgaben | tasks with a short code – create them and run them with one click |
| 🚦 Milcrid Dialog-Regeln | what Milcrid suggests when a known window asks something |

**Milcrid Editor:** a simple text editor like Notepad on Windows. Several files sit in **tabs**; a **●** on a tab means
"not saved yet". Program files (e.g. .py) are shown with line numbers, normal text with line wrapping – the button
**„Zeilenumbruch"** (line wrap) switches. Unsaved text survives a restart. Closing an unsaved tab asks „verwerfen?"
(discard?) – only a second click closes it.

**Milcrid Bildbetrachter (image viewer):** shows an image fitted to the window. The **mouse wheel** zooms at the spot
you point at; drag to move the image; **double-click** switches between "see all" and original size. **◀ ▶** browse all
images in the same folder. **⟲ ⟳** rotate; **Speichern** (save) writes the rotation into the file, **Speichern unter**
(save as) creates a new one.

**Milcrid Video:** plays videos (MP4, WebM) and music (MP3, OGG, WAV, FLAC). **⏮ ⏭** jump to the previous or next file
in the folder; for music, "next automatically" is on by default. Speed and full screen as well. If a format can't be
played (e.g. AVI), **„In VLC öffnen"** (open in VLC) appears. Closing the window stops playback; minimizing keeps music
playing.

## 10. The file manager

**Datei Manager** shows the folders and files on your PC, similar to Explorer on Windows. You can open several at once.

- **Open a folder:** single click. **Open a file:** double-click – it opens with its default program.
- **Top:** ◀ ▶ back and forward, ▲ one level up, the address bar (every part clickable), the **search field** for the
  open folder, and sorting.
- **Column headers** Name, **Geändert** (modified), **Art** (type), **Größe** (size): one click sorts, a second reverses.
  Folders are always on top.
- **+ Neuer Ordner / + Neue Datei** (new folder / new file): type a name, press Enter.
- **Bottom:** how many entries there are and what you have selected.
- **Left:** at the top **💾 Laufwerke** (drives, see below), then 🏠 Home and the folder tree: ▶ expands a folder.
- **Right-click on a free area** (also far down) offers new folder, new file and paste.
- Files can be dragged into a theme with the mouse.

### The right-click menu

| Entry | What happens |
|---|---|
| Öffnen (open) | with the default program |
| Öffnen mit ▸ (open with) | list of programs that can handle the file – the default one is golden |
| Bearbeiten (edit) | small text field right under the file – to view and change quickly |
| Eigenschaften (properties) | name, type, size, date, path |
| Kopieren · Ausschneiden (copy · cut) | to paste in another folder (right-click free area › **Einfügen**, paste) |
| Umbenennen (rename) | the name is already selected, the extension stays |
| Duplizieren (duplicate) | copy with a new name in the same folder |
| Löschen (delete) | moves to the trash – can be restored |

**Delete permanently**, bypassing the trash: Shift+Del – Milcrid asks first.

### The trash (Papierkorb)

What you delete in the file manager – or Milcrid deletes at your request – goes to the **Milcrid Papierkorb**. Open it:
in the file manager on the left **🗑 Papierkorb**, via the tile under "Meine Apps", or say *„öffne Papierkorb"*.

- The list shows name, **original location**, when deleted, and size – newest first.
- **↩ Wiederherstellen** (restore, or Enter) puts it back in its old place. If a file with the same name is there now,
  the restored one is called "Name (2)" – nothing is ever overwritten.
- **Endgültig löschen** (delete permanently, or Del) and **Papierkorb leeren** (empty trash) ask first.
- Select several: Ctrl+click, all: Ctrl+A. Right-click works too.
- Things deleted on a USB stick stay on the stick (in a hidden folder) and appear in the trash while the stick is plugged in.

The trash still takes up disk space. Only „Papierkorb leeren" frees it.

### USB sticks and external drives

Plug in the stick or drive, click **💾 Laufwerke** (drives) on the left of the file manager. Milcrid mounts everything
plugged in and shows it with name, size and free space. One click opens the drive – from then on everything works as in
Home: open, copy, cut, rename, delete, also between Home and the stick (copy, then on the stick right-click a free area ›
**Einfügen**, or Ctrl+V). If nothing is plugged in, it says so – plug in and click 💾 Laufwerke again.

**Before unplugging:** right-click the drive › **„Sicher entfernen"** (safely remove). Only when „… kann jetzt abgezogen
werden" (can now be unplugged) appears at the top is everything written. If the stick is still in use (e.g. an image from
it is open), Milcrid tells you – close the window, try again.

Milcrid itself (the AI) only works in your Home folder – only the file manager accesses sticks, when you operate it.

## 11. Opening files and default programs

When you open a file – in the file manager, in a theme, via a button, or by telling Milcrid *„öffne …"* – Milcrid decides
by the **file type** which program gets it. Set this under **Einstellungen › Standardprogramme** (Settings › default programs):

| File type | Default |
|---|---|
| Text (.txt, .md) and program code (.py …) | Milcrid Editor |
| Web pages, PDF | Firefox |
| Images | Milcrid Bildbetrachter |
| Videos | Milcrid Video |
| Music | VLC |
| Documents, spreadsheets, presentations | LibreOffice |
| Archives (.zip …) and everything else | Linux decides |

Only programs that can really handle the file type are offered – including ones you install later.
**„Alle auf Vorgabe"** (all to default) resets everything. If a file doesn't open at all, Milcrid tells you at the top
centre of the screen.

## 12. Themes, programs

**Themes:** a **Thema** is your own collection – e.g. "Haushalt" (household), "Rechnungen" (bills) or "Auto" (car).
It holds files and programs that belong together. Create themes in the **Themen Manager**, drag files from the file
manager into them, and open them later with one click or with *„öffne Thema …"* (open theme …). Several themes can be
open at once. **Tip:** don't give a theme the same name as yourself – the AI misunderstands *„öffne Thema Klaus"* more
easily than *„öffne Thema Privat"*.

**Programs:** under **Meine Apps** you find all programs. **„Vom PC (Linux)"** (from the PC) shows what is installed
(Firefox, LibreOffice …); **„Neu einlesen"** (re-scan) adds newly installed ones. Start a program with one click, with
*„öffne …"*, or via a shortcut. Programs that are not part of Milcrid open in their own window.

## 13. The AI

**Local AI:** Milcrid's AI is a **language model** running on your PC. Under **Lokale KI › Models** you see which models
are installed and can switch with one click. Larger models know more but answer more slowly.

**What the AI may do:** under **Lokale KI › Fähigkeiten** (abilities) and **C26SO › Sicherheit** (security) you decide
what Milcrid may do on its own – open programs, close windows, open files, search the internet, and so on. Whatever is
switched off is not even offered to the AI.

**What Milcrid remembers:** under **Profil Manager › KI Profil** there are five areas, each on **one line**:
**Anzeigen · Kopieren · Löschen · an/aus** (show · copy · delete · on/off).

| Area | What's in it |
|---|---|
| 🪞 Selbst (self) | the **Fundament** (foundation – Milcrid's core values, only you change them) and the **Selbstbild** (self-image) that Milcrid keeps writing itself |
| 📔 Tagebuch (diary) | how Milcrid experienced a conversation – one entry after each saved chat |
| 🧾 Erfahrungs-Log (experience log) | what went wrong technically and how it was solved |
| ⏱️ Kurzzeitgedächtnis (short-term memory) | the latest summaries – Milcrid reads them at every start |
| 🗄️ Langzeitgedächtnis (long-term memory) | all older summaries and saved chats word for word |

- **Anzeigen** (show) opens the content right below, newest first. Click again = hide.
- **Kopieren** (copy) and **Löschen** (delete) only work while the content is shown – you always see what you copy or delete.
- **Löschen** asks first and moves everything to the trash – restorable. For **Selbst**, only the self-image is cleared; the foundation stays.
- **an/aus** (on/off): whether Milcrid keeps writing there.

**Online AI (optional):** **Milcrid Online KI** connects you to an internet AI such as Gemini or Claude – using your own
access key. The connection is **always off at start**. Only when you switch it on in the window does anything go to the internet.

## 14. Security and privacy

- The local AI works **without the internet**. Conversations stay on your PC.
- Milcrid only searches the internet when you explicitly ask for it (*„such im Internet nach …"*).
- **Shut down, restart** and **deleting by voice** only happen after a confirmation question and your „ja" (yes).
- The AI does not open Milcrid's own program files and memory in other programs.
- Under **C26SO › Sicherheit** there is a **Notbremse** (emergency brake).

## 15. Updates, restart and shutdown

**Updates:** when updates are available, Milcrid shows a small note at start. Under **Update** you see Linux, programs,
the AI service and drivers separately. Drivers and kernel are deliberately **not** updated automatically. For the AI
service the old version is kept; **„Zurück"** brings it back if something is wrong.

**Shut down / restart:**

- **Shut down:** golden dot far right – or say *„PC aus"* (PC off).
- **Restart:** golden dot far left – or say *„PC Neustart"* (PC restart).
- A small window opens. If the **text of the online AI** is not saved yet, it first asks **„Speichern?"** (save?) –
  **Speichern / Nicht speichern** (save / don't save). Open programs like LibreOffice are listed below – they ask
  themselves whether to save when they close.
- Then: **Neu starten** / **Ausschalten** (restart / shut down) or **Abbrechen** (cancel) – by click or by voice:
  „ja" (yes), „nein" (no), „speichern", „nicht speichern", „abbrechen".
- Opened by voice, the window stays for 30 seconds. Once it is closed, the question is over – a late „ja" does nothing.
- After switching on, Milcrid starts by itself.
